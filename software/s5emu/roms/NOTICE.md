# The emulator's ROMs

Each ROM here is HBIOS: Mike Riley's 2005 BIOS for ELF/OS with Ken Rother's System-5 section
(`firmware/hbios/NOTICE.md` says which part is whose). `hbios-1.0.4` is made by `../tools/mkrom.py`; `hbios-1.0.5`,
`hbios-1.0.6` and `hbios-video-g` by the `build.py` in their `firmware/hbios/` folders.
