# MEM3-E pre-build review, 2026-10-30

A full review of Memory Rev E (MEM3-E) before Ken builds it: the boards were ordered from JLCPCB on 2026-10-20 (order 2000765A-Y55, 4 layers).
It was done in three independent passes over the netlist, board file and BOM (logic, electrical, board and build), then checked and combined.

**Verdict: no blockers.** The ordered board is the board in this folder, and every pin of every chip is correct.
The decode does what it should, timing has wide margins at 1.76 MHz, and the footprints, jumper labels and silkscreen agree with the netlist.
What remains is part choices, notes for the next revision, and housekeeping.

## Before building: part choices

| # | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Superseded 2026-10-31 (`REVIEW-2026-10-31-independent.md`): keep the 74LS32 and 470 Ω.** Was: IC14: fit a 74HCT32 instead of the 74LS32**, and make **R2 and R4 1k** instead of 470 Ω | IC9 (CD4042B) drives A15 and /A15 into IC14 pins 2 and 5. An LS input can draw 0.4 mA low; a CD4042B is only guaranteed 0.51 mA at a 0.4 V low at 25 °C, less when warm. A15 is the bank select, so a weak low could select the wrong bank. A 74HCT32 is pin-compatible and draws µA. IC14 also sinks LED2 (RAM LO, R2) and LED4 (EPROM LO, R4): 1k keeps that current within HCT's 4 mA rating (about 3 mA: dimmer, still visible). IC12 can stay a 74LS32: nothing CMOS-weak drives it. With the LS32 the card will most likely work too; this removes the margin question. |
| P2 | **EPROMs: 27C256 (CMOS), not NMOS 27256 and not 28C256** | The BOM says "27256". An NMOS 27256 draws about 100 mA. A 28C256 EEPROM has A14 on pin 1 and /WE on pin 27, so in this socket it would lose half its range, and writes in its window could reprogram it. VPP (pin 1) is on VCC, as a 27C256 needs (the reason the old Rev B misreads Ken's Microchip 27C256). Any speed grade works (more than 1.5 µs of margin). |
| P3 | **RAM: 62256 in the 600 mil package** (e.g. AS6C62256-55PCN, or HM62256LP-70 / CY62256-70PC) | The footprints (DIL28-6) are 600 mil wide. The "skinny" 300 mil 62256 will not fit. |
| P4 | **RN1–RN4: 4.7k bussed 10-pin SIL** (e.g. Bourns 4610X-101-472LF), common pin 1 at the "1" end | Pin 1 is the common pin on VCC, pin 2 is unused, pins 3–10 pull up the mux inputs. An isolated network (4610X-102) would be wrong. |
| P5 | **Logic:** CD74HCT151E ×4 (not 74LS151: README), CD4042BE ×2, CD4050BE ×2 (keep the B-series CD4050B: timing finding C1) | Already on the BOM; the suffixes are what to order. |
| P6 | **Not on the BOM:** 28-pin 600 mil sockets ×4, 16-pin ×8, 14-pin ×2, two 2×8 and two 1×8 headers, 16 shunts | |

## Checked and correct

- **Every IC pin** (62256, 27C256, 74HCT151, CD4042B, CD4050B, 74LS32 ×2): address, data, /CE, /OE (= -MRD via IC8), /WE (= -MRW via IC8), power and ground. No floating inputs: unused CD4050B inputs and IC12's spare gates are grounded, and every mux data input has its 4.7k pull-up.
- **The decode**, run at gate level from the netlist with several jumper maps: a chip's /CE goes low only when -VMA is low, A15 picks its bank and its jumper for that 4K block is fitted. Each block gives one chip or none; with -VMA high nothing is selected. **The Rev B chip-select fault is fixed** (strobes grounded, bank select ORed into each /CE). An open jumper keeps its block empty, so **blocks 7 (the 6845 card) and F (the CPU card's EPROM) stay free** with the default map.
- **The latch:** CD4042B POL = VCC, clock = TPA: transparent while TPA is high, holds from its fall. Correct for the 1802's high address byte.
- **Bus behaviour:** the card drives the data bus only when a chip is selected and -MRD is low; during an 1802 OUT that is correct (it is a memory read); never during a write.
- **Timing at 1.76 MHz (worst case):** RAM read +2256 ns, EPROM read +2126 ns, write set-up +1185 ns, CD4042B setup +246 ns and hold about +94 ns. The card is good to **about 2.2 MHz**; the CD4042B setup time sets the limit. The 74HCT151 changes nothing that matters (at most +15 ns).
- **Levels and loading:** the bus address and control lines come from the 1802 (CMOS, rail to rail) or the bus driver (CMOS), so the CD4050B's 3.5 V input threshold is no problem. The data bus load is under 1 mA.
- **Power:** about 55 mA typical, 140 mA max. Every power pin is in its plane (57 GND, 43 VCC); one 0.1 µF (0.1" spacing) per IC, 4.6–11 mm from its VCC pin, which is fine with solid planes; 10 µF bulk.
- **Board:** the ordered zip equals this board and passes the pre-order checks (layers 4 = 4 = 4, DRC, unconnected, parity, netlist 88/88, both planes). Outline 141.0 × 96.24 mm and the bus connector position are the same as memory Rev B, BUSDRV-B and 6845 Rev B. Every DIP has pin 1 at its right-hand end. **The jumper labels agree with the wiring**, column by column.
- **LEDs:** LED2–LED5 each light only with their own chip (RAM LO, RAM HI, EPROM LO, EPROM HI), about 6 mA; LED1 is power.

## Notes (no action needed to build)

- **N1 – Jumpers:** each column is ROM row / GND / RAM row, so one shunt can enable RAM or ROM in a block, never both. Only a wire could enable both, and then they would fight on the bus.
- **N2 – LED polarity mark is weak:** both LED pads are round, with only a small silk tick by the cathode. **Fit each LED with its flat side and short lead to the left hole.** For the next revision: a square cathode pad or a "+".
- **N3 – Off LEDs may glow faintly** with the 74LS32 (its high level is about 3.4 V). With a 74HCT32 they are fully off.
- **N4 – INP into RAM** (the IDE card's data held until memory /WE rises) has only +49 ns, because /WE comes through IC8 (CD4050B, up to 140 ns). A 74HC4050 for IC8 only would gain about 100 ns here but adds about 13 ns to C1. Ken's call; the CD4050B is fine at 1.76 MHz.
- **N5 – C1 start-of-cycle contention** (known, `hardware/TIMING-REVIEW.md`): about 228 ns at 1.76 MHz, noise only; the planes help.
- **N6 – C8 (10 µF)** sits about 2.4 mm from the top edge, like BUSDRV-B's C3: check it clears the card guide.
- **N7 – Floating bus inputs with no bus master:** with the CPU card out and the bus driver not yet master, -MRD, -MRW, TPA and A0–A7 float into CMOS inputs. -VMA floats high into the 74LS32, so no chip is selected and nothing is written; the cost is only extra current. **For bus Rev D (not yet ordered): consider 10k pull-ups on -VMA, -MRD and -MRW** (and a pull-down on TPA).
- **N8 – Chip labels** sit inside the DIP outlines (visible with open-frame sockets); the latch and buffer labels say "4042" and "4050" (buy CD4042BE / CD4050BE).
- **N9 – No `cottageworker.com`** on this board; it is already ordered, so it goes on the next revision unless Ken asks.

## Build and bring-up checklist

**See also `REVIEW-2026-10-31-independent.md`** (IC14 stays a 74LS32; extra build cautions).

1. **Bare board:** check U$1 pin 1 (VCC) to pin 30 (GND) is not a short (a plane short is invisible on a 4-layer board); continuity pin 1 → IC1 pin 28, pin 30 → IC1 pin 14.
2. **Solder, lowest first:** R1–R5 (R2, R4 = 1k if IC14 is a 74HCT32), RN1–RN4 (common pin to the "1" end, right), the 0.1 µF caps, the sockets (every notch to the right, toward the LEDs), the headers, C8 (+ on the left), the LEDs (flat side and short lead to the left), U$1 last.
3. **Before any chip:** VCC–GND still open; every socket's pin 1 at the right-hand end.
4. **Power with no chips:** LED1 lights; +5 V at each socket's VCC pin (pin 28 on the 28-pin sockets, 16 on the 4042/151 sockets, 14 on the 74LS32 sockets, **pin 1 on the CD4050 sockets**); about 6 mA.
5. **Fit the logic** (IC2/4/13/15, IC6/9, IC7/8, IC12/14): about 20–30 mA, nothing warm after a minute. Keep it short with no bus master (the CD4050 inputs float).
6. **Fit the memories:** IC1 (RAM LO) and IC5 (EPROM HI, programmed on the T48) for the default map; IC11 and IC3 optional.
7. **Jumpers for RRRRRRR-EEEEEEE-:** LO block: GND–RAM in columns 0000–6000 (the seven right-hand columns; 0000 at the far right), 7000 (far left) open. HI block: GND–ROM in columns 8000–E000, F000 (far left) open.
8. **Bring-up** with BUSDRV-B, CPU card out: `python3 bringup.py memory` (the default `--mem-rev D` is the same circuit), `--rom <image> --rom-base 8000` to check the EPROM, `--full` for every RAM byte, then `bringup.py all` with the CF and 6845 cards. On the simulator it gives 6 pass, 1 warn (blank EPROM), 0 fail.
